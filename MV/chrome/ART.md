# CHROME

A 90-second sample. The 1980s as neon and a hand. Picture only, for a general YouTube gaming cyberpunk audience.

Bulbs and the neon sign carry magenta and cyan. The Walkman and the disks keep a warm hand, with no chromatic split on the skin. Night traffic is the street the sign is above. A Commodore 64 is a prop in that night, not a product photo on a white sweep.

The score stays with this cut. Shot lengths sit on a 3-second bar. `python3 build_samples.py chrome` from `MV/`.
