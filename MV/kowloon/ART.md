# KOWLOON

A 90-second sample. Hong Kong at night, for a general YouTube gaming cyberpunk audience. The picture only.

Crushed black, cyan in the water, magenta on the towers, a small RGB split on the signs and the train. The rainy Sham Shui Po sign is the human-scale shot. The harbor is the wide shot. Do not pass daylight, a zoo, or a talking head off as the city.

The score stays with this cut. Shot lengths sit on a 3-second bar. `python3 build_samples.py kowloon` from `MV/`.
