# NINETY

A 90-second sample. The 1990s as a game still running. Picture only, for a general YouTube gaming cyberpunk audience.

The Amiga landscape is graded to a green CRT, with scanlines. Fiber reads as a field of light. The Game Boy sits in a black void, screen blank, so no game we do not own is on it. Skin on the disks stays warm and unsplit.

The score stays with this cut. Shot lengths sit on a 3-second bar. `python3 build_samples.py ninety` from `MV/`.
