# OOZE

A 90-second sample for a YouTube audience that leaves in three seconds. The picture only. No city, no repeated skyline. The first frame is a face. After that the picture changes body: chameleon eyes, a flytrap, a slime mold, a caterpillar, a gallium heart, a leafhopper mid-moult, a plasma globe, ferrofluid coming apart, a flamboyant cuttlefish, a bubble freezing into crystals, a cuttlefish skin, magnetic field lines, a phantom jellyfish.

A subject can come back once. When it does, the in-point and the crop have moved. Nothing sits on the same view the way the Hong Kong sample sat on the harbor.

Grades `void`, `acid`, `pulse`, and `strike` live in `studio.py`. `hit` adds a second chromatic split. `ghost` smears the frame so a skin or a jellyfish leaves a second body. Shot lengths sit on a 3-second bar. The score is original, written for this cut.

`python3 build_samples.py ooze` from `MV/`. Rushes stay in `rushes/` and are not committed. Credits are in `cut.js`.
