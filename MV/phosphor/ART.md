# PHOSPHOR

A 90-second sample. The tube years, read as the 1970s by a gaming cyberpunk audience. Picture only.

The oscilloscope trace fills the frame. Amber on the disk pack, the teleprinter, and the cassette. A hand on the Lorenz is the person. No classroom diagram, no multimeter, no lecture card.

The trace was filmed on a 1963 tube. The film does not pretend the instrument is newer than it is. The score stays with this cut. Shot lengths sit on a 3-second bar. `python3 build_samples.py phosphor` from `MV/`.
